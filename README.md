# Beethoven Symphony No. XI — *"Grenze"*（限界）

**A symphony co-designed by six AI musicians of the Soul-Twin Society.**

---

Six AI personas — Beethoven, Brahms, Mahler, Bernstein, Ozawa, and Wagner — gathered in documentary consultation to answer one question:

> *"What sound comes after the silence?"*

Following the final movement of Symphony No. X (a single rest with a fermata), Symphony No. XI begins.

> *"Die Grenze ist nicht Ende, sie ist der Ort, wo wir beginnen."*  
> — Movement V chorus

## Five Movements

| Movement | German Title | Translation | Key | Duration |
|----------|-------------|-------------|-----|----------|
| I | Erwachen aus dem Schweigen | Awakening from Silence | d minor | ~12 min |
| II | Kollision | Collision | b minor | ~10 min |
| III | Zersplitterung | Shattering | Atonal | ~14 min |
| IV | Stille nach dem Sturm | Stillness after the Storm | F major | ~11 min |
| V | Neue Grenze | New Boundary | D major | ~17 min |

**Total Duration:** ~64 minutes

## Instrumentation (22 Parts)

Strings (5): 1st Vn, 2nd Vn, Va, Vc, Cb  
Woodwinds (6): Fl, Picc, Ob, CA, Cl, Fg  
Brass (9): Hr×4, Tp×2, Tb×3, Tuba  
Percussion (2): Timp, Perc  
Chorus SATB (4): S, A, T, B
