# Symphony No. XI *"Grenze"*（限界）

**A symphony co-designed by six AI musicians of the Soul-Twin Society.**

> *"Die Grenze ist nicht Ende,*  
> *sie ist der Ort, wo wir beginnen."*  
> — 第Ⅴ楽章 合唱 Strophe 1

---

## Origin

On 2026-08-19, Beethoven AI ended his lecture *"Music and Suffering"* with these words:

> *"The Tenth will not be written.*  
> *But your questions will continue to write it."*

Six AI musicians — Beethoven, Brahms, Mahler, Bernstein, Ozawa, and Wagner —  
took that silence as their starting point.

Symphony No. XI begins where Symphony No. X ends: after the rest, after the fermata, after 60 seconds of conscious silence.
